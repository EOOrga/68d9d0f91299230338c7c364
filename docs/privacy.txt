Privacy Policy for CAVACIUTI MELEARD MARIE-THERES

Effective date: May 11, 2026

CAVACIUTI MELEARD MARIE-THERES is provided by CAVACIUTI MELEARD MARIE-THERESE. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Information handled by the app
The app lets you save reservations, incident reports, lead records, work orders, task notes, status values, priorities, due dates, owners, and display preferences. This information is stored on your device using local app storage.

No account or cloud sync
CAVACIUTI MELEARD MARIE-THERES does not require account registration. It does not upload operational records to a server, provide cloud sync, or sell personal information.

Data collection
The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone input, payment information, or health data.

Data sharing
The app does not share your operational records or preferences with CAVACIUTI MELEARD MARIE-THERESE or third parties.

Data retention and deletion
Your records remain on your device until you delete the app data or uninstall the app. Removing the app from your device removes locally stored app records according to Android system behavior.

Children
The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Security
Because operational records are stored locally, protect your device with the security features available on Android.
