The Android app keeps the day practical: create a record, assign an owner, set priority, review status, and move work forward without account setup or cloud tools.

I Incidents

L Leads

W Work orders

Built for fast local use

Designed as an app first, with short paths for the repeated operational checks that happen throughout the day.

1

Create the record

Choose reservation, incident, lead, or work order, then add owner, priority, notes, and due window.

2

Review the board

Use the dashboard, filters, and status lanes to see what needs attention next.

3

Advance the status

Move records through scheduled, in progress, and complete states as work is handled.

Private by design

Records and preferences are stored locally on the Android device. The app does not require account registration, does not provide cloud sync, does not collect analytics, and does not connect to external services.
